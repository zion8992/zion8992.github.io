I have way too many projects to be doing this, but I decided to start a Newsletter.  
I call it "The Cubyz Newsletter". A newsletter about my favorite game, [Cubyz](https://github.com/pixelguys/cubyz).

The idea is simple: every week, release a short newsletter with random things about Cubyz: jokes & memes, facts and other stuff.
