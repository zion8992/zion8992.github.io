## Introduction

The world in 2026 is built and designed in with the philosophy that people want something in return for what they give to others. 

Human beings have the requirement of *currency* to operate in life. Every Human need is locked behind a paywall and is though to fight it.  
The reason for this is that people want something in return for what they give.

But we, ourselves, have proven that we don't need anything in return. There are millions of organizations out there to solve every little problem in the world: hunger, war, water, comfort, etc...  
We are just showing ourselves that what most people go after early in life is *pointless*.

One of the biggest concerts that ever happened was "**Live Aid**" in 1985. The reason I'm using this as an example is simply because Queen, Dire Straights, U2 and 69 other bands went to go play for a total of 1.9 billion people during, in total, *16 hours*. This big event served as a fundraiser to end hunger in Africa and raised **$125 \* million** dollars. Aside from most of these bands having fortunes and just wanting the free publicity, most people that made Live Aid possible were working in disaster zones and there were also millions of people who donated without actually attending to the concert.

This all just to say, that we should **Do things not because you will get something in return, but instead because it will help someone(s)**. It's the law of Love. And as Humans, we must embrace that or die without ever truly feeling Human: Because **Loving is Human**.

Simple?

## The "Society 2.0"

Now, you might be wondering, how exactly will this "Libre Society" work? Well the idea is to "**Take one step back and two steps forward**", rather than just taking one step forward and it being Humanity's last. What I mean by "Take on step back" is, basically, go back like 200 years or so. 

The way this society will work will never be able to sustain plastic, oils or any of that. Because in a world without money, everyone will want to go and explore their life and do things they want to do. **Nobody wants to spend hours in a factory assembling phones or computers**.

That makes you wonder, "Why not just stay in the current world if it will give us a greater chance of evolving?". Well evolution will happen, just in the beginning, it will be evolution we already know about and read in history books. But then it will excel at a greater speed than ever. If nobody stays in a factory assembling phones, we *will* have more minds to think, more engineers to design eco-friedly power plants and rockets. We already know how feed millions of people, but at the same time "Destroy their childrens lives", so why can't we have those same million people, but in a way that is good for our environment?

Now, feeding millions of people is complex. It will require power and things that we won't initially have, but that is because you are thinking big. **Baby steps**. You are most likely thinking of a large city like New York, where all you see is gray. But now envision a prarie with a few farms next to the river and people working on those farms to feed themselves. Envision a large pot filled with boiling soup, that will feed the 60 people that will live in that village.

The problem is we will first need to destroy New York (by "New York", I mean destroy the current grey world.[^1] [Read footnote 1]). This we can do with the technologies that we currently have. But then how will we build a power dam with only wood and sticks?[^2] [Read footnote 2]. That's why we have to act fast before we have another billion people to feed, hydrate, heat, clothe and love.
