In January of 2026, `Cubyz 0.0.0` got released. Ever since a lot has happened, more specifically Ironite.

Every since the release, I got very interested in Cubyz. So interested that I decided to become a volunteer for the [wiki team](https://wiki.cubyz.net).

After becoming a volunteer for the wiki team, I was wandering around the [ashframe](https://ashframe.net) website. Ashframe is a small organization that hosts various games like [Minecraft](https://minecraft.net), the new (and boring) [Hytale](https://hytale.com) and Ashframe + Ashframe Legacy which are [Cubyz](https://cubyz.net) servers.

After seeing the Ashframe server list (which was the official one for the game), I though, it needs a re-design. Cubyz was a relatively new game and a lot of work was still needed, and as a person that gets bored of projects because they *are* boring, I thought of making a server list of my own.
